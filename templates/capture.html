Photo Guidelines

  • Take a selfie in natural daylight, facing a window
  • Keep your face directly facing forward
  • Ensure good lighting without harsh shadows
  • Remove masks, sunglasses, or face coverings
  • Keep a neutral expression with minimal makeup
  • Avoid direct sunlight and artificial lighting
  • Do not upload group photos - only ONE face allowed

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. Your photos are processed locally in your browser and on our secure server. We do not store, share, or use your images for any purpose other than providing you with your skin tone analysis. All images are deleted from the server when you end the analysis or start a new one.
